# Abstract Examples Index

All abstract example cites should point to the local files below.

1. Problem/Gap -> Contribution -> Evidence
`Use when readers first need to understand the practical or technical gap, then the paper's central contribution, then the evidence supporting it.`
`references/examples/abstract/template-a.md`
2. Observation/Insight -> Contribution -> Evidence
`Use when the paper is motivated by a surprising measurement, formal property, user behavior, workload pattern, or design insight.`
`references/examples/abstract/template-b.md`
3. Multiple Contributions -> Evidence and Scope
`Use when the paper makes two or three distinct contributions and must also delimit where the claims hold.`
`references/examples/abstract/template-c.md`
